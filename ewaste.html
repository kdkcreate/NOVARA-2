WHAT IS E-WASTE?

Technology doesn't end when we stop using it.

E-waste is discarded electrical or electronic equipment: phones, computers, chargers, screens, batteries and appliances.

WHY IT MATTERS

Devices contain useful materials—and can create risks when handled badly.

Resources

Electronics contain metals, plastics and other materials that can sometimes be recovered.

Data

Back up, sign out and reset a device before handover.

Health

Unsafe informal recycling can expose workers and children to hazardous substances.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Keep, repair, reuse—then recycle responsibly.

Maintain devices, repair where feasible, donate working items, protect your data and use authorised collection or recycling channels.
