E-waste in India & Kerala.

As technology use grows, convenient collection, awareness and responsible handling become even more important.

India Kerala
◎
India

A challenge—and a circular opportunity.

Rapid technology adoption, short device lifecycles and mixed handling systems make awareness vital.

↗

Scale

Households, offices and IT hubs generate a growing volume of end-of-life electronics.

⌘

Systems

Authorized recyclers, collection channels and producer take-back schemes are expanding.

✦

Opportunity

Informed choices can move devices into formal recycling and reuse streams.

Kerala

Local action can lead the way.

▣

Schools & colleges

Collection drives, repair cafés and component libraries can turn learning communities into practical changemakers.

⌂

Urban communities

Municipalities can partner with authorized recyclers and build awareness at ward level.

Before you hand over a device

Separate it from household waste, back up your data, reset it, and use an authorized collection or recycling channel.
