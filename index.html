Small actions, real impact

A clearer route from drawer to recovery.

Keep old devices in circulation where possible, then send the rest to an authorized recycler—not the household bin.

01

Pause before you toss

Identify the device, accessories, and battery. If it still works, repair, reuse, donate, or sell it first.

02

Protect your data

Back up what you need, sign out of accounts, and reset devices before handing them on.

03

Choose a safer exit

Use an authorized collection point or certified recycler so materials can be recovered responsibly.

Know before you decide

Your device has value beyond the screen.

Components contain recoverable metals and materials—but unsafe handling can harm people and the planet.
